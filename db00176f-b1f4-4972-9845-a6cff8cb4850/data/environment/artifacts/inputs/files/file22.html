Profile

Steady hands, clear standards

I have spent my career in human resources because I wanted to understand how organizations treat the people who depend on them, and I thought someone should know how to do it right. That conviction has carried me from a coordinator role on a manufacturing floor to the director's chair.

Today I oversee human resources for a manufacturing and distribution company headquartered in Nashville, leading four managers across three offices. My work runs from benefits enrollment and a new employee wellness program to chairing the diversity and inclusion committee. Accuracy beats speed, proper channels beat shortcuts, and follow through is the whole job.

Leadership is not a position, it is influence, and influence is earned one kept commitment at a time.
  • Integrity firstConfidential matters stay confidential. Fairness is documented, not improvised.
  • Data informedEvery initiative is tied to a business outcome and measured against it.
  • People centeredA circle of safety so employees give more, take better risks, and stay.
  • ConsistentCulture changes slowly and only when leaders hold the line over time.
