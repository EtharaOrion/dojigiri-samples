Gulf Coast Ballet Presents

An Evening of Dance

The annual spring gala benefiting the company and its dancers

Saturday, March 20, 2027  |  The Houstonian Hotel, Houston

Reception and silent auction at six, seated dinner and performance to follow. Chaired by Patricia Hargrove.

The Evening

An Original Program, Created for One Night

Each spring our patrons gather for the company's most important evening of the year. Proceeds support the extra rehearsal weeks, the apprentice positions, and the new choreography that define our seasons. Our goal this year is 450,000 dollars.

Six o'clockChampagne reception and silent auction in the courtyard
Seven o'clockSeated dinner in the ballroom
Eight o'clockPerformance by company principals
The finaleA new pas de deux choreographed by Daniel Brooks
