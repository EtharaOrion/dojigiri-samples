# Memory: Carlos Moore

## Personal Profile
Carlos Moore is a poised, community-rooted man at a midlife inflection point: career proud but quietly plateauing, marriage stable but settling into routine, children grown and gone, and a mother who needs more of him every year. He earned a BS in Business Administration from Ridgemont University in 1996 and an MBA there in 2004 through the evening program while working. His faith is central and lived rather than preached; he attends Calvary Hill Church every Sunday and sings in the choir, and his spirituality shows up in how he treats people. He is married to Maria, together 28 years and married 26. He fills the quiet of the empty nest with overwork, committee volunteering, and home projects he does not always enjoy. He believes in doing things the right way through proper channels and has little patience for shortcuts or people who game the system.

## Work & Projects

HR Manager at Pinnacle Staffing Solutions, Nashville (July 2001 to August 2007, completing his MBA evenings). Senior HR Manager at Cumberland Health Systems, Nashville (September 2007 to December 2017). Corporate HR Director at Bellmark Industries since January 2018.

Currently at Bellmark Industries, a manufacturing and distribution company headquartered at 2200 Commerce Park Boulevard, Nashville, TN 37214. He oversees HR for roughly 400 employees across three offices, with four HR managers reporting to him, and he in turn reports to Wanda Giles. Works 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM weekdays with occasional evenings for employee events or urgent situations. Current focus: rolling out a new employee wellness program (his flagship effort), annual benefits enrollment in October, and chairing the DEI committee. He is also quietly navigating layoff planning for one underperforming division, which is confidential. Work stress runs moderate to high. He is weighing SHRM-SCP recertification credits and attending a regional HR conference in Memphis.
